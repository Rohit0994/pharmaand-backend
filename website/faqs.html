Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about our company, our products, and how we work with patients and partners.

What does pharmaand GmbH do?

pharmaand GmbH ("pharma&") is a privately owned specialty pharmaceutical company headquartered in Vienna, Austria. We are dedicated to preserving the availability of essential and specialty medicines worldwide. We acquire, develop, and commercialise established medicines — "breathing new life into proven medicines."

When was pharma& founded and who runs it?

pharmaand GmbH was founded in 2017 in Vienna, Austria. The company is led by Managing Directors Frank Rotmann and Elmar Zagler.

Which countries does pharma& operate in?

pharma& operates in 15+ countries through its headquarters in Vienna and subsidiaries in Austria (Loba biotech), Canada, Croatia, Germany, Spain, France, India, Italy, Switzerland, the UK, and the US. Corporate offices are also located in Zug (Switzerland), Zagreb (Croatia), and Paris (France).

What medicines does pharma& market?

Our portfolio includes over 10 specialty therapeutics. Key products include Pegasys® (peginterferon alfa-2a, hepatitis B & C), Rubraca® (rucaparib, ovarian cancer), Farydak® (panobinostat, multiple myeloma), Levact® (bendamustine, haematology), Anafranil® (clomipramine, psychiatry), Asacol® (mesalazine, IBD), Emselex® (darifenacin, urology), Euphyllin® (theophylline, respiratory), and the pipeline asset satraplatin (oncology).

How do I report a side effect or adverse event?

Please contact our pharmacovigilance team at pv@pharmaand.com. You should also report adverse events to your national regulatory authority. Do not delay seeking medical advice from your healthcare professional.

I'm a healthcare professional — how can I request medical information?

Contact our medical information team at medinfo@pharmaand.com (outside the US) or medinfo.us@pharmaand.com (US). Please identify yourself as a healthcare professional in your message.

How can I partner with pharma&?

We welcome partnerships with manufacturers, distributors, and other organisations that share our commitment to patient access. Please contact our business development team at bd@pharmaand.com.

How does pharma& approach innovation?

We focus on lifecycle innovation: reformulating established medicines, extending their clinical value through new indications or delivery systems, and ensuring modern manufacturing standards. A notable example is our acquisition of Loba biotech, which manufactures the active pharmaceutical ingredient for Pegasys®.

How can I contact pharma& media relations?

For press enquiries please email media@pharmaand.com.
