Help Center

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about our company, our products, and how we work with patients and partners.

What does pharma& do?

We safeguard the availability and quality of established medicines worldwide, while investing in lifecycle innovation to keep them relevant for modern healthcare.

Where are your products available?

Our medicines are supplied across many countries through a network of distribution partners, hospitals, and pharmacies. Availability varies by market — please contact your healthcare provider or our team for specifics.

How do I report a side effect?

Please speak to your healthcare professional first, and report the event via your country's pharmacovigilance system. You can also notify our medical safety team using the contact form.

I'm a healthcare professional — how can I request medical information?

Our medical affairs team is available to answer scientific and clinical questions about our products. Please use the contact form and indicate that you are a healthcare professional.

How can I partner with pharma&?

We welcome partnerships with manufacturers, distributors, and other organisations that share our commitment to patient access. Reach us via the contact page to start a conversation.

How does pharma& innovate on existing medicines?

We focus on lifecycle innovation: improved formulations, modern manufacturing, broader regulatory access, and better patient experience — all rooted in proven therapies.

Are you hiring?

We're always interested in talented people who share our mission. Please reach out via the contact form for current opportunities.

Where can I find prescribing information?

Healthcare professionals can request the relevant prescribing information for our products through their local market. Contact us for assistance.
