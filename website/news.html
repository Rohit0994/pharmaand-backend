Newsroom

News & updates

Stay up to date with announcements, partnerships, and insights from across our organisation.

2025

New packaging facility in Scheibbs, Austria

pharma& opened a dedicated packaging facility in Scheibbs, Austria, strengthening its European supply chain capabilities and supporting local manufacturing.

2024

Acquisition of Satraplatin

pharma& acquired satraplatin, an oral platinum-based anticancer agent with potential in castration-resistant prostate cancer, expanding our late-stage oncology pipeline.

2023

Acquisition of Rubraca® (rucaparib)

pharma& secured worldwide rights to Rubraca®, a PARP inhibitor indicated for BRCA-mutated advanced ovarian cancer, further strengthening its oncology franchise.

2022

Acquisition of Farydak® (panobinostat)

pharma& acquired Farydak®, an HDAC inhibitor for relapsed and refractory multiple myeloma, from Secura Bio Ltd.

2021

Acquisition of Pegasys® and Loba biotech

A landmark milestone: pharma& acquired worldwide rights to Pegasys® (peginterferon alfa-2a) and the API manufacturer Loba biotech GmbH, creating a fully integrated hepatitis franchise.

2017

pharmaand GmbH founded

pharmaand GmbH was established in Vienna, Austria with a mission to preserve access to essential medicines for patients worldwide. The company was founded by Frank Rotmann and Elmar Zagler.
