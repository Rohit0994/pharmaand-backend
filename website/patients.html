For Patients

Putting patients first

Behind every product is a person who depends on it. Our work is shaped by their stories, their needs, and their trust.

Reliable access

We work to ensure your medicine is available where you need it, when you need it. If your healthcare provider has prescribed one of our products, we want it to be there.

Quality assurance

Our medicines are manufactured under rigorous international quality standards, with full traceability from production to pharmacy.

Reporting side effects

If you experience an adverse reaction, please contact your healthcare professional and report it via your local pharmacovigilance system. You may also reach our medical safety team directly.

Important note

The information on this site is for general educational purposes only and is not intended to replace medical advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Always consult your doctor or pharmacist about your treatment.

Patient support

For specific questions about a product you are taking, please speak to your healthcare provider in the first instance. For other queries, our medical information team is available via the contact page.
