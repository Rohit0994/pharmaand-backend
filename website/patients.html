For Patients

Putting patients first

Behind every product is a person who depends on it. Our work is shaped by their stories, their needs, and their trust.

Reliable access to your medicine

pharma& works to ensure that specialty medicines — including oncology, hepatitis and other specialty therapies — remain available to patients worldwide. We are dedicated to preventing supply shortages for medications patients depend on.

Quality you can trust

Our medicines are manufactured under stringent international quality standards with full traceability from production to pharmacy. Loba biotech GmbH, our subsidiary in Austria, manufactures the active pharmaceutical ingredient for Pegasys®.

Report an adverse event

If you experience a side effect while taking a pharma& medicine, contact your healthcare professional immediately and report it to your national pharmacovigilance authority. You may also notify our safety team at pv@pharmaand.com.

US patients — Heritage Biologics & Summit SD

In the United States, pharma& products are made available through joint venture partners Heritage Biologics, LLC and Summit SD, LLC, which manage market access and patient services for the US market.

Medical information

Healthcare professionals and patients seeking medical information about pharma& products can contact our medical information teams. Please use the appropriate regional email listed on our Contact page.

Complaints

Product quality complaints should be directed to complaints@pharmaand.com. We treat all complaints seriously and follow a structured quality investigation process.

Important medical disclaimer

The information on this site is for general educational purposes only and is not intended to replace medical advice from a qualified healthcare professional. Always consult your doctor or pharmacist about your treatment.
