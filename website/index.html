Our Mission

Enduring availability. Reliable quality. Trusted medicines.

We safeguard the long-term supply, dependability, and quality of medicines that patients and healthcare providers depend on every day around the world.

Through partnerships with global manufacturers, regulators and healthcare systems, we extend the lifecycle of established therapies and ensure they remain accessible for the patients who need them.
