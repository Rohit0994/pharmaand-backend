About Us

Who we are

A global pharmaceutical company committed to keeping established medicines available, dependable, and accessible to patients everywhere.

Our story

pharma& was founded with a simple but powerful idea: that the world's established medicines deserve careful stewardship. As global priorities shift, many trusted therapies risk being overlooked. We exist to ensure these medicines remain available — manufactured to high standards, delivered reliably, and continuously improved through thoughtful innovation.

Our values

  • Patients first — every decision begins with the people who depend on our medicines.
  • Reliability — predictable quality and supply, every time.
  • Stewardship — protecting the future of essential treatments.
  • Partnership — close collaboration with healthcare systems worldwide.
  • Integrity — operating with transparency and responsibility.

Leadership

Our leadership team brings decades of experience across pharmaceutical operations, regulatory affairs, manufacturing, and commercial strategy. Together, we're building an organisation that puts the long-term needs of patients ahead of short-term goals.

Global presence

From our headquarters to manufacturing partners and regional offices, our network spans dozens of countries — ensuring patients receive medicines wherever they live.
