About Us

Breathing New Life into Proven Medicines

pharmaand GmbH (pharma&) — a privately owned global company headquartered in Vienna, Austria, dedicated to preserving essential medicines worldwide.

Who We Are

A global force for essential medicine availability

pharmaand GmbH (pharma&) is a privately owned global company headquartered in Vienna, Austria. Since our inception in 2017, we have been dedicated to preserving the availability and fostering the further development of essential medicines worldwide to leave no patient behind.

Whether a former blockbuster or niche medicine, we have acquired the worldwide rights to and integrated 10+ high-need specialty therapeutics.

Our global network of partners, combined with our presence in 15 countries with a geographic focus on Europe and the U.S., spans all pharmaceutical functions, making us an integration powerhouse across the development continuum.

Our Portfolio

Portfolio of Essential Medicines

Acquisition

pharma&'s agility enables rapid scaling. Bold acquisitions include Pegasys® (peginterferon alfa-2a) and Rubraca® (rucaparib), expanding our portfolio and capabilities.

Integrated Organisation

With subsidiaries, joint ventures, and partners, pharma& is fully integrated — spanning R&D, manufacturing, distribution, and patient access services.

Therapy Areas

Our portfolio spans hematology, oncology, cardiovascular, infectious disease, and more — with an increasing focus on hematological and oncological treatments.

Global Reach

Present in 15 countries across Europe and the U.S. with a global partner network ensuring patients receive medicines wherever they live.

Our Journey

Exceptional Growth Since 2017

2017 — Early Days: Corporate formation and build-up of European partner network.

2018–2021 — Off-Patent Portfolio Acquisition: Anafranil®, Androskat®, Asacol®, Dridase®, Emselex®, Euphyllin®, Faktu®, Levact®.

2021 — Integration Powerhouse: Pegasys® acquisition from F. Hoffmann-La Roche Ltd. (Roche) and Loba biotech acquisition.

2022 — Farydak® Acquisition: Commercial building block begins with UK commercial structure. pharma& affiliates initiated.

2023 — Rubraca® Acquisition: Onboarding of U.S. and Europe commercial, medical, pricing and market access, and development talent.

2024: pharma& acquires global rights to satraplatin from Dayton Therapeutics. Promotional agreement with Tolmar for certain Rubraca® U.S. indications.

2025: pharma& expands development as its secondary packaging facility begins operations in Scheibbs, Austria.
