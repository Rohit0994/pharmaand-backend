Innovation

New life for proven medicines

Innovation isn't only about discovering new molecules — it's also about reimagining how trusted therapies can serve patients better.

How we innovate

Lifecycle innovation

Reformulation

Updated dosage forms, improved tolerability, and patient-friendly delivery — extending the value of established medicines.

New indications

Exploring additional therapeutic uses for proven molecules through clinical research and partnerships.

Manufacturing excellence

Investing in modern processes, quality systems, and sustainable production at our manufacturing partners.

Digital health

Connecting medicines with adherence support, patient resources, and outcome tracking tools.

Regulatory science

Working with authorities globally to maintain and expand access to essential treatments.

Patient-centric design

Listening to patients and clinicians to identify improvements that genuinely matter in everyday treatment.

Why this matters

Many established medicines remain the cornerstone of treatment in their fields — but as commercial focus shifts toward newer entrants, there's a real risk that proven therapies stagnate. Our innovation work ensures these medicines continue to evolve in step with modern healthcare needs: easier to take, safer to handle, and available where they're needed most.
