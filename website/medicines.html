Medicines

Trusted therapies, dependable supply

Therapies that healthcare providers and patients have relied on for decades — and that we're committed to keeping available.

Our approach

Guarding the foundation of modern medicine

Established medicines remain the backbone of clinical practice across most therapy areas. We focus on protecting their availability — through reliable manufacturing, careful regulatory stewardship, and a long-term commercial mindset.

For each medicine in our portfolio, we partner closely with manufacturing sites, distributors, and healthcare authorities to ensure continuous supply, transparent quality, and responsive customer service.

Therapy areas

Where we focus

Oncology & Hematology

Rubraca® (rucaparib) — PARP inhibitor for BRCA-mutated ovarian cancer.
Farydak® (panobinostat) — HDAC inhibitor for multiple myeloma.
Levact® (bendamustine) — for chronic lymphocytic leukaemia and NHL.
Satraplatin — pipeline oral platinum agent for prostate cancer.

Infectious Disease

Pegasys® (peginterferon alfa-2a) — gold-standard treatment for chronic hepatitis B and C. pharma& holds worldwide rights (excluding Japan and US).

Psychiatry & CNS

Anafranil® (clomipramine) — tricyclic antidepressant used in depression and obsessive-compulsive disorder.

Gastroenterology

Asacol® (mesalazine) — for ulcerative colitis and Crohn's disease.
Faktu® — topical treatment for haemorrhoidal disease.

Urology

Emselex® (darifenacin) — selective M3 antagonist for overactive bladder.
Dridase® (oxybutynin) — anticholinergic treatment for overactive bladder.
Androskat® — for erectile dysfunction.

Respiratory

Euphyllin® (theophylline) — bronchodilator for asthma and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).
